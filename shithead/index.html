House rules

  • Everyone gets 4 face-down, 4 face-up and 4 in hand.
  • Swap time: before play, trade hand cards with your face-up cards.
  • Play a card equal or higher than the pile. Same-rank cards can go down together.
  • Keep your hand topped up to 4 from the deck while it lasts.
  • 2 resets: anything can go on it.
  • 7 is the mirror: it copies the card underneath it.
  • 10 bombs the pile. Four of a kind bombs it too. Bomb it and you go again.
  • Can't play, or don't want to? Pick up the pile any time on your turn.
  • Hand first, then face-up, then flip your face-down cards blind.
  • The last player holding cards is the Shithead.

Install on your phone

iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

It gets its own icon, opens full screen, and solo games work offline.
